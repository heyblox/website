To stop a serial returner on Shopify, use Shopify's return rules to limit what they can send back, then block the customer so their next order never ships. Shopify has no native way to stop a customer ordering again, so the block needs a customer blocklist app like [Blox](https://apps.shopify.com/blocklist), which holds or cancels a blocked customer's future orders before fulfillment.

Returns are part of selling online, and most customers who return are not a problem. This guide is for the small group who are: the shopper who buys five sizes and keeps none, wears an item and sends it back, or claims a refund on every order.

## What serial returning and refund abuse look like

Merchants usually spot some mix of:

- A customer who returns most of what they buy, order after order
- "Wardrobing": an item is used, then returned as if new
- Refunds claimed for items that were received, or returns that arrive empty or with a different item
- Repeated "missing" or "damaged" claims from the same person
- A refund dispute that turns into a chargeback
- The same person coming back under a new email after you cancel or refuse a return

The common thread is a repeat person. A return policy handles one return at a time; it does not remember who keeps abusing it.

## What Shopify offers natively

Shopify gives you good tools for controlling individual returns:

1. **Return rules.** In your store's policy settings you can set a return window, return shipping fees, restocking fees, and mark items as final sale. This limits what any customer can return.
2. **Return request approval.** When customers request returns through their account, you can review each request and approve or decline it, instead of accepting every return automatically.
3. **Order history, tags and notes.** You can look at a customer's past orders and refunds, and tag them (for example `serial-returner`) so staff notice next time.

Where these fall short:

- **There is no native way to stop a customer ordering again.** Return rules apply to everyone; they do not single out the person abusing them, and declining a return does not stop the next purchase.
- **Tags and notes depend on someone noticing** before the order is fulfilled.
- **A new email starts fresh.** A tag on one customer record does not follow the person to a new account.

So the native tools reduce the damage of each return. To stop the person, you need to block them.

## How to stop serial returners with Blox

Blox is a customer blocklist for Shopify. It shows you which customers are costing you money, you decide who to block, and Blox stops their future orders before they ship.

### 1. Install Blox

Install [Blox: Block Customer Blacklist](https://apps.shopify.com/blocklist) from the Shopify App Store. Your past orders and chargebacks are imported on install, with nothing to set up.

### 2. Find your worst customers

Blox shows you which customers are costing your store the most, so customers who return most of what they buy stand out. Start with the worst offenders.

You can also search any identifier you already have, such as an email, phone number or address.

### 3. Check the whole profile

Customers who use several emails or accounts appear as one profile. Before you block, you see every account that person uses and their full order and refund history across them.

### 4. Block them with the reason "Return abuse"

Block the customer and choose **Return abuse** as the reason. Other reasons include chargebacks, discount abuse, support abuse, missing or lost packages, fraudulent orders, unauthorized reseller, and other. The reason keeps a record your team can understand later.

The block applies to the whole profile, so a new email does not get around it.

### 5. Hold or cancel their next order

Once a customer is blocked, their future orders are automatically held for review or cancelled before fulfillment, depending on your settings. You never ship to them again, and there is nothing for staff to remember.

### 6. Watch for community flags

When another Blox store blocks a customer, other Blox stores are flagged about that customer. If a shopper blocked elsewhere for return abuse orders from you, you can review them before you ship. The community blocklist is included on every plan, even Free.

## Native tools vs a blocklist for serial returners

| Goal | Shopify native | Blox |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Find customers who refund the most | Manual review of order history | See who costs your store the most |
| Stop the customer ordering again | No native option | Future orders held or cancelled before fulfillment |
| Stop them on a new email | No | One profile per person, across emails and accounts |
| Learn from other stores | No | Community blocklist, every plan |

Most stores use both: Shopify's rules to control each return, and a blocklist for the few customers who should not get another order.
